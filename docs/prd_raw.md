
Draft PRD Notes:
Wealth Companion (working name) App
Idea / user
High Net worth individuals have money spread across many places, but no single view that really helps them understand where they stand or think clearly about decisions. Existing tools feel either retail or bank-controlled. This should feel more like a financial co-pilot than an app.
User is a US high net worth individual, already working with banks or advisors, very sensitive to trust and privacy, not price driven.
What feels broken
Getting a clear picture still requires spreadsheets and calls. Simple questions take effort:
Where am I exposed (US vs global)?
How liquid am I if I need cash soon?
What happens in a market downturn?
Possible directions (very early)
Some kind of net worth view, maybe an asset mix view, maybe simple scenarios. Possibly AI to help explain things in plain language, but not for advice.
Data / AI / constraints
Probably pulls from brokerage and bank accounts. Illiquid assets are messy. Offshore assets maybe later.
AI could summarize or explain, but can’t give advice and must be explainable.
Security and privacy are critical. Regulation exists but isn’t fully thought through. This must not feel like a retail app. Accuracy matters more than speed.
Business model / success (unclear)
Could be subscription, via advisors, or white-label. Not decided.
Success is users checking it regularly and trusting it as their reference point. Metrics unclear.
Non-goals
Not replacing advisors. Not mass market. Not trading.
